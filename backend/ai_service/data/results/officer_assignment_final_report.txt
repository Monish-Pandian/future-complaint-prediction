OFFICER ASSIGNMENT FINAL REPORT
============================================================

EXPERIMENT OVERVIEW
-------------------
This module evaluates risk-aware officer assignment strategies using historical
backtesting with simulated officers. The assignment decisions were fixed BEFORE
joining future outcomes, ensuring no data leakage.

Setup:
- 3 verification budgets: 5%, 10%, 20% (90% exploitation / 10% exploration)
- 2 assignment policies: RISK_ONLY vs RISK_DISTANCE_WORKLOAD
- 4 random seeds for robustness: 42, 123, 2024, 999
- Synthetic officer pool (LOW capacity): 273 officers, 7 departments
- Total candidate locations: 13,770 (across all budgets)
- Assignment rate limited by officer capacity (~489 slots total)

POLICIES COMPARED
-----------------
1. RISK_ONLY_ASSIGNMENT: Choose eligible officer with lowest current workload,
   break ties by distance. (Weights: risk=1.0, distance=0.0, workload=0.0)

2. RISK_DISTANCE_WORKLOAD_ASSIGNMENT: Normalized composite score.
   (Weights: risk=0.4, distance=0.3, workload=0.3)

Both respect: department compatibility, officer availability, capacity limits.
Unassigned locations recorded with reason.

ANSWERS TO KEY QUESTION
------------------------

"Can risk-aware officer assignment reduce travel and workload imbalance 
while preserving the escalation-discovery benefits of the validated 90/10 
exploration policy?"

5% BUDGET:
  - Reduces travel: YES (15% less total distance, 15% less mean distance)
  - Reduces workload imbalance: NO (4.5% WORSE imbalance, though not statistically significant)
  - Preserves escalation discovery: YES (identical escalations found, precision/recall unchanged)
  - VERDICT: Travel reduction achieved, but workload imbalance slightly worse at this budget.

10% BUDGET:
  - Reduces travel: YES (14% less total distance, 14% less mean distance)
  - Reduces workload imbalance: YES (6.7% improvement)
  - Preserves escalation discovery: YES (identical escalations found)
  - VERDICT: Both travel and workload imbalance reduced while preserving discovery.

20% BUDGET:
  - Reduces travel: YES (9% less total distance, 9% less mean distance)
  - Reduces workload imbalance: YES (2.8% improvement)
  - Preserves escalation discovery: YES (identical escalations found)
  - VERDICT: Both travel and workload imbalance reduced while preserving discovery.

RECOMMENDATION
--------------

RECOMMENDED POLICY: RISK_DISTANCE_WORKLOAD_ASSIGNMENT for 10% and 20% budgets.
For 5% budget, either policy is acceptable (travel reduction without discovery loss).

EVIDENCE:
1. Across all budgets, RISK_DISTANCE_WORKLOAD reduces total travel distance by 9-15%.
2. At 10% and 20% budgets, it also reduces workload imbalance (6.7% and 2.8%).
3. At 5% budget, workload imbalance slightly increases (4.5%) but this is within
   the noise (std ~0.04) and not statistically significant.
4. Escalation discovery is IDENTICAL across all budgets and seeds - no loss in
   precision, recall, or discovery rate.
5. The ablation shows the full Risk+Distance+Workload strategy outperforms 
   Risk-only on distance at all budgets, and on workload imbalance at 10%/20%.

COMPLEXITY CONSIDERATION:
The full strategy (Risk+Distance+Workload) has only marginally more complexity
than Risk+Distance (both use 3 normalized components vs 2). Since the full
strategy performs better on workload imbalance at the budgets where it matters
most (10%, 20%), and the complexity difference is minimal, the full strategy
is recommended over a simplified Risk+Distance variant.

LIMITATIONS (CRITICAL)
----------------------
1. SYNTHETIC OFFICERS ONLY: All officer identities, departments, availability,
   workloads, capacities, and home areas are SIMULATED. These do NOT represent
   real Chicago personnel, union rules, shift schedules, or qualifications.

2. SIMPLIFIED CAPACITY MODEL: The LOW capacity scenario provides only ~489
   assignment slots for 13,770 candidate locations (3.6% assignment rate).
   Real departments would have different staffing levels.

3. STATIC AVAILABILITY: Officer availability is fixed per simulation run, not
   varying by week or shift. Real availability is dynamic.

4. NO GEOGRAPHIC RESTRICTIONS: Officers can be assigned to any community area
   regardless of distance. Real deployments have geographic constraints.

5. SIMULATED DEPARTMENT COMPATIBILITY: The SR-type-to-department mapping is
   a simplified simulation, not based on actual Chicago departmental structure.

6. NO REAL-WORLD DEPLOYMENT CLAIM: This is a HISTORICAL BACKTEST for
   methodological validation only. Results should not be used for operational
   officer deployment decisions.

7. SINGLE EXPLORATION RATIO: Only the validated 90/10 exploration policy was
   tested. Other exploration ratios may yield different assignment dynamics.

8. XGBOOST MODEL FIXED: The prediction model was not retuned for assignment.
   Joint optimization of prediction + assignment could yield different results.
