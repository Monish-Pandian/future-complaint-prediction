================================================================================
BUDGET-CONSTRAINED OFFICER VERIFICATION EXPERIMENT REPORT
EXPLOITATION vs EXPLORATION FOR FEEDBACK-BIAS REDUCTION
================================================================================

1. RESEARCH QUESTION
------------------------------------------------------------
Does explicitly allocating part of a limited officer-verification budget
to exploration reduce feedback-selection bias compared with sending
officers only to the highest-risk predictions?

2. DATASET
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Chicago 311 service requests
Test period: 2025 (52 weeks)
Community areas: 77
Complaint types: 10
Total candidate observations: 39270
Average candidates per week: 770.0

3. ESCALATION TARGET DEFINITION
------------------------------------------------------------
escalation_target = 1 if future_complaint_count >= 1.5 * rolling_mean_4_weeks
escalation_target = 0 otherwise
Valid rows: 39270
Positive escalations: 9143 (23.3%)
Negative: 30127 (76.7%)

4. VERIFICATION BUDGET DEFINITION
------------------------------------------------------------
For each week, verification_budget = ceil(N_candidates * K)
Budgets tested: K = 5%, 10%, 20%

5. EXPLOIT_ONLY POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------
For each week: rank all candidates by predicted escalation probability,
select top K% for verification.

6. EXPLOIT_EXPLORE POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------
90% exploitation / 10% exploration split
Exploitation: top 90% of budget slots by predicted risk
Exploration: random 10% from remaining candidates (seed=42)
No overlap between exploitation and exploration selections

7. SIMULATION METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------
HISTORICAL BACKTEST / SIMULATED FIELD VERIFICATION
For each selected candidate, compare predicted risk with actual
next-week historical outcome (future_complaint_count).
actual_escalation = escalation_target (evaluation only)
DOES NOT represent real physical officer inspection.

8. LEAKAGE SAFEGUARDS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Selection uses ONLY prediction-time information:
  predicted_probability, community_area, sr_type, week_start,
  historical features, rolling features, spatial features
- NOT used for selection: future_complaint, future_complaint_count,
  escalation_target, any future information
- Chronological weekly cycles (no random splits)
- Reproducible random seed = 42 for exploration

16. LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Fixed 90/10 split not optimized
- Escalation target based on rolling_mean_4_weeks availability
- Chicago-specific patterns may not generalize
